BACKGROUND

My route into software development was not a straight line. I spent years in customer service and hands-on technical support before returning to school to make programming the focus of my career. Those earlier roles taught me to listen carefully, investigate what actually happened, and explain a solution without making it harder than it needs to be.

At Geek Squad, and now through Insight Global, I have worked directly with Windows, macOS, Linux, hardware, and business applications. Troubleshooting those systems made debugging feel practical rather than theoretical: start with the symptoms, test assumptions, document the result, and make sure the person using the system can move forward.

At ECPI University, my coursework started with programming fundamentals and Python before moving into advanced object-oriented development with Java and C#. I have also studied JavaScript and web interface design, SQL and advanced databases, server-side development with PHP, and two levels of mobile application development.

The program has also introduced me to Linux administration, operating systems, cybersecurity, cloud solutions, systems analysis and design, and applied project management. I have completed my software development capstone and am now finishing the final requirements for my B.S. in Software Development. Alongside school, I have continued learning through NVIDIA AI, Dell, and Apple training. I am ready to bring that combination of coursework and support experience to a software team.
